Our new paper, **“Validating sensory conflict theory and mitigating motion sickness in humans with galvanic vestibular stimulation,”** is now published! This work provides experimental evidence for the role of sensory conflict in motion sickness and demonstrates how GVS can reduce symptoms during motion.
