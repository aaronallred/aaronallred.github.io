Our new journal paper, **“Validating sensory conflict theory and mitigating motion sickness in humans with galvanic vestibular stimulation,”** is now published! This work provides experimental evidence for the role of sensory conflict in motion sickness and demonstrates how GVS can reduce symptoms during motion.

Read the full paper [here](https://www.nature.com/articles/s44172-025-00417-2).
